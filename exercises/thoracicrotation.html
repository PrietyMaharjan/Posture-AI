Thoracic Rotation

4 mins
Core

Improves core mobility and thoracic extension to counteract hunching from desk work.

How to do it

  1. Stand with your back flat against a wall, feet a few inches out.
  2. Raise your arms to a goalpost position, elbows and wrists touching the wall.
  3. Slowly slide your arms up and down, keeping contact with the wall throughout.
